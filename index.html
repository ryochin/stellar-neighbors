Coordinates and the galactic plane

By default the XYZ axes follow galactic coordinates: the XY plane is the galactic plane (latitude b = 0°) and the Z axis points to the north galactic pole. Switch to J2000 equatorial coordinates to compare the same stars against another reference frame.
